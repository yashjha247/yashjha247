## How I learn and build

I don't want to just generate code and move on. I want to understand what I'm building.

My approach is simple:

1. Start with what I currently understand.
2. Research the fundamentals.
3. Form a hypothesis about how things work.
4. Experiment with a small implementation.
5. Debug, test, and investigate what goes wrong.
6. Explain what I learned and decide what to explore next.

I use AI to investigate concepts, explore solutions, and accelerate implementation. But I want to remain responsible for understanding the code, testing its behaviour, and making engineering decisions.
